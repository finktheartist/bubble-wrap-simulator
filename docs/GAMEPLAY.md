# Gameplay video

[Watch the MP4](gameplay.mp4) · [Play the game](https://bubble-wrap-simulator.vercel.app)

A 43-second browser recording with the game's own audio. The video shows all nine tools, the moving targets, physics, and impact effects in one continuous take.

| --- | --- |

Capture uses the actual standalone production build from commit `d448573`, with scripted camera direction and tool inputs. The rendered UI, tool animations, physics outcomes, and sound come from the game. The MP4 is 1280 × 720, encoded at 30 fps with H.264 video and AAC stereo audio; the game runs at its real capture frame rate.

The editable Blender model library and export scripts are included in [the asset workflow](../art/README.md).
